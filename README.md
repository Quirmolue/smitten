# Smitten Kitchen Catering Services

> Memorable food for the moments people remember.

Smitten Kitchen Catering Services is a concept and project space for a warm, modern catering brand. It is designed to make planning an event feel as inviting as sharing a meal: browse thoughtfully prepared menus, imagine the atmosphere, and make an inquiry without getting lost in logistics.

Whether the occasion is an intimate dinner, a workplace gathering, or a full celebration, the project centers generous seasonal food, personal service, and effortless hospitality.

## What this project is for

This repository provides the foundation for a catering-services website and its supporting content. The finished experience should help prospective clients:

- Discover the brand's food philosophy and event approach.
- Explore sample menus and catering formats.
- Find services that suit their occasion, group size, and style.
- View event inspiration and relevant service details.
- Submit a clear, low-friction catering inquiry.

## Main features

### A clear service story

Introduce Smitten Kitchen's personality, culinary point of view, and the occasions it caters. Visitors should quickly understand what makes the service feel distinct: seasonal menus, generous presentation, and tailored planning.

### Menus made for browsing

Present breakfast, lunch, dinner, cocktail, and dessert selections in a way that is both inspiring and practical. Menu pages can call out dietary accommodations, package options, and seasonal availability.

### Catering for every gathering

Organize offerings around real event needs, including private dinners, weddings, corporate events, celebrations, and drop-off catering. Each service can explain what is included and what makes it a good fit.

### Inquiry-first booking flow

Give clients a simple way to share their event date, location, guest count, budget range, and vision. A well-designed inquiry flow turns an initial idea into a useful conversation with the catering team.

### Helpful planning details

Answer common questions about lead times, dietary needs, staffing, rentals, delivery, and service areas so clients can plan with confidence before reaching out.

## Suggested site structure

```text
Home                A welcoming overview and invitation to inquire
About               Brand story, food philosophy, and team
Services            Catering formats and event types
Menus               Sample seasonal menus and dietary information
Gallery             Food, tablescapes, and event inspiration
FAQ                 Practical planning answers
Contact / Inquiry   Event details form and next steps
```

## Content principles

Keep the voice warm, specific, and appetizing. Focus on what a guest will experience rather than only listing services. Pair evocative food descriptions with practical details such as serving style, guest range, and dietary options.

Good catering copy feels like an invitation: confident enough to guide a client, but personal enough to make their event feel one of a kind.

## Contributing

When adding pages or content, favor accessible design, responsive layouts, clear calls to action, and imagery that reflects the care behind the food. Keep menu and service information easy to scan on both desktop and mobile.
